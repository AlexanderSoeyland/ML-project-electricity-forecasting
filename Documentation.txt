For EDA: 

For day_ahead_price, I would not interpolate it.
 The target has 977 missing observations, 
 but the gaps are short (maximum 9 hours). 
 Simply remove those rows when constructing the final modelling dataset. 
 You still retain well over 98% of your target observations.

For load and hydro, interpolation is very reasonable. 
The gaps are almost entirely 1–4 hours, so linear time interpolation should 
introduce negligible distortion


Wind is different, the wind results are:

995 missing total
largest gap = 742 hours ≈ 31 days
second largest = 48 hours
then 23, 23, 23 hours

I would not interpolate the 742-hour gap. Linear interpolation over an entire month would
 essentially invent a month's worth of wind forecasts, which is not defensible.
Instead, only interpolate short wind gaps, for example up to 6 consecutive hours
